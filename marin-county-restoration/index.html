What Makes Marin Restoration Different

  • Creeks and tides — Corte Madera Creek, San Anselmo Creek and the bayfront flood in big storms, especially when rain lines up with high tide.
  • Hillside homes — Lower levels built into the hill take on groundwater, and steep lots complicate access and equipment.
  • Fog and shade — Southern Marin and the canyons stay damp, so wet materials grow mold quickly.
  • Wildland-urban interface — Much of Marin borders open space, with wildfire smoke and ember risk every fire season.
  • Eichlers and historic homes — Terra Linda and Lucas Valley Eichlers and older Ross Valley homes each need specific drying and repair methods.
