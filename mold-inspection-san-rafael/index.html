What We Look for in San Rafael Buildings

Local building types have their own trouble spots:

  • Terra Linda and Lucas Valley Eichlers — Low-slope roofs, radiant heat in the slab and large single-pane glass mean roof leaks, slab leaks and window condensation. Mold often hides behind wall panels and in ceilings.
  • Canal neighborhood apartments — Older multi-unit buildings near the tidal canal with high groundwater and crowded units tend toward bathroom, closet and window-wall mold.
  • Gerstle Park, Dominican and downtown older homes — Raised foundations, original plumbing and limited insulation lead to crawlspace and wall-cavity mold.
  • Hillside homes in Sun Valley and Peacock Gap — Lower levels against the hill can take on water through walls and slabs during winter storms.
  • Real estate transactions — Buyers and sellers want a clear, documented answer before closing. We provide a report that stands on its own.

How a San Rafael Mold Inspection Works

  1. Interview and history — Leaks, odors, symptoms, past water damage and renovations.
  2. Visual inspection — Every room, the attic, crawlspace, under sinks and around windows and HVAC.
  3. Moisture mapping — Meters and thermal imaging to find wet materials you cannot see.
  4. Sampling — Air and surface samples where they add information, analyzed by an accredited third-party laboratory.
  5. Written report — Findings, photos, readings and lab results, plus a remediation scope if one is needed.
Inspection first, then decide. If mold is found, the report gives a clear scope of work. If you choose us for the cleanup, see mold remediation in San Rafael. Post-remediation clearance testing confirms the job is done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need air testing if I can already see mold?

Not always. If growth is visible and its cause is clear, removal can proceed without air testing. Testing is useful when there is odor but no visible source, after a water loss, for real estate transactions, or when someone has health concerns.

How long does a mold inspection take?

Most San Rafael homes take one to two hours on site. Lab results typically come back in two to three business days.

Can you inspect a rental unit for a tenant or landlord?

Yes. We document conditions objectively with photos, moisture readings and lab results, which helps both sides agree on what needs to be fixed.
