Where We Actually Find Mold in San Rafael

Mold in San Rafael concentrates in a small number of predictable places. These are the ones we open first:

  • Canal District ground-floor units — Low elevation, tidal influence and dense multi-family occupancy
  • Eichler radiant slabs in Terra Linda — Failed in-slab heating loops saturate from below with no visible source
  • Any assembly that was 'left to dry' — Marin humidity means passive drying does not finish the job — most local mold is a drying failure
Why San Rafael buildings hold moisture: San Rafael is Marin's largest city and its restoration profile is shaped by proximity to the bay, hillside terrain, and a Canal District that sits at sea level.
