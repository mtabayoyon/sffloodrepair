Fire and Smoke in Sonoma Valley Buildings

What makes Sonoma fire and smoke work different:

  • Wildfire smoke intrusion — Fine wildfire smoke gets into attics, ductwork, insulation and soft contents even when the house is closed up. It needs HEPA cleaning and filtration, not just airing out.
  • Historic Plaza buildings — Adobe, stone and old-growth wood interiors cannot take aggressive chemical cleaning. Dry ice blasting and controlled methods protect original material.
  • Wineries and hospitality — Tasting rooms, inns and restaurants need fast smoke and odor removal to reopen. We schedule around business operations.
  • Rural properties and outbuildings — Homes in Glen Ellen and Kenwood often have barns, studios and guest units that need assessment along with the main house.
  • Kitchen and electrical fires — Year-round, most structure fires start in kitchens, dryers and older wiring.
Smoke without fire: if your home smelled of smoke after a nearby wildfire, check your homeowners policy — smoke damage is often covered even when the house did not burn. We document soot and odor so your adjuster can see it. See also fire damage restoration across Sonoma County.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you clean wildfire smoke out of my house?

Yes. We HEPA-vacuum and clean surfaces, clean or replace HVAC filters and ducts, treat odor with hydroxyl generators, and clean or pack out soft contents.

Do you work on historic buildings around the Sonoma Plaza?

Yes. We use dry ice blasting and low-moisture methods that remove soot without damaging adobe, plaster or original wood.

Do you handle rebuilding after a fire?

Yes. After cleanup and demolition, our reconstruction team rebuilds to pre-loss condition so you work with one company and one estimate.
